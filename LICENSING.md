The rulesets (`AzerothPrestige/Data/Ruleset_v*.lua`) and the other data tables are code: every client must be able to read
them to recompute anybody's points ([ADR 0003](docs/adr/0003-points-recomputed-from-events.md)).

### The name is not licensed (GPL-3.0, section 7(e))

The licence does not grant rights to the name **"Azeroth Prestige"** or to the project's identity. A modified version
that you distribute to others must have a different name, must say that it is modified, and must not present itself as
the official addon or be published under this project's name on addon sites. Distributing the unmodified official release
is not affected.

## Artwork and sounds: all rights reserved

The logo, the rank badges, the category emblems, the historic seals, the profile frames, the particles and the sounds are
the visual and audio identity of the project. They are **not** under an open licence. You may use them as part of the
addon, show them in screenshots, videos and streams, and propose improvements to this project; anything else needs
written permission. The full terms are in [`LICENSE-CONTENT.md`](LICENSE-CONTENT.md).

A version of the addon built from the GPL code must therefore ship **its own artwork and sounds**, unless the owner gives
written permission.

The artwork and sound files are a **separate work** distributed together with the GPL program, under their own terms. The
media folder of the addon carries a `NOTICE.md` (from milestone M3) that says so, names the licence and the owner
(Finland6738) and links [`LICENSE-CONTENT.md`](LICENSE-CONTENT.md).

Shapes drawn by the project's own tools (the placeholder badges of `tools/make_badges.py`, the icons of
`tools/make_media.py`) are generated by GPL code and covered by the code licence until the final artwork replaces them.

The fonts that ship with the addon (from milestone M3) are under the SIL Open Font License 1.1, each with its licence
text. The libraries (Ace3, LibDataBroker, LibDBIcon) keep their own licences and are added by the packager. The list,
