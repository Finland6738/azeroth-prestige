# Azeroth Prestige

*Italiano: [[Azeroth Prestige|Home-it]]*

![Azeroth Prestige logo](../../assets/logo-source.png)

**Prestige ranks, badges and leaderboards for World of Warcraft: Forever.** Azeroth Prestige rewards what you complete in the game with **Prestige Points** in eight categories, and shows your rank as a badge to the other players who run the addon.

> **Status: in design.** The addon is not available yet. This manual grows with every milestone: each page arrives with the feature it describes. The plan is in the [roadmap](https://github.com/Finland6738/azeroth-prestige/blob/main/ROADMAP.md).

## What it will do

- **Eight categories**: Vanguard (PvP), Delver (dungeons and raids), Pathfinder (exploration), Adventurer (quests and reputation), Diplomat (helping and mentoring), Artisan (professions), Merchant (trade), Steward (events and guilds). A ninth progression, **Global Prestige**, sums them with a bonus for variety.
- **A ladder of 22 tiers**, from Wood to Dragonite, each with the divisions IV (entry) to I (top). Tiers are permanent: they never go down.
- **Seven apex ranks**, Master, Grandmaster, Challenger, Titan, Keeper, Aspect and **Legend**, each with a points threshold and **at most 100 seats** per category, assigned again at every weekly reset. A seat can be lost to others, but every title you held stays in your roll with its dates.
- **Badges** on nameplates, tooltips, unit frames and chat, a **profile** at three levels, a **history** of every point, **leaderboards** and the **Hall of Prestige**.
- **Celebrations** when you rank up, never during combat, a boss or a conversation with an NPC.

## What it will never do

- Give a gameplay advantage, or let anyone filter groups by rank: it measures commitment and variety, not skill.
- Count time spent: only completed activities count.
- Count interactions between your own characters, or points earned while a player 10 or more levels above you carries you.
- Send anything outside the game. There is no server: data travels between players with the addon, and the leaderboards show what that network knows.

## Languages

The addon will speak **English** (the default) and **Italian**, and more languages can be added later. This manual is in both: every page has an Italian twin.
